Membership

Any currently enrolled student can join, regardless of experience level. We have three divisions — Competitive Roster, Casual & Social, and Content & Broadcast — so there's a lane whether you're chasing a ranked title or just want a relaxed game night.

Membership itself is free. Some events, such as away tournaments, may involve a small cost-sharing contribution, which is always announced in advance and never required to remain a member.

Yes. Divisions describe how you'd like to participate, not a permanent label. Message the committee any time and we'll update your membership record.

Events & Attendance

Only Competitive Roster members have attendance expectations, tied to their team's practice schedule. Casual & Social and Content & Broadcast members can join whichever events fit their time.

Events are posted on the club's announcement channel and shared during weekly meetups. New members are added to the announcement channel right after their application is confirmed.

Just let the event organiser know as early as you can, so a spot can be freed up for someone on the waitlist. There's no penalty for giving reasonable notice.

Code of Conduct

Respect for opponents, teammates, and organisers at all times. Toxic behaviour, harassment, or unsportsmanlike conduct of any kind is not tolerated and may result in suspension of membership.

Equipment & Access
