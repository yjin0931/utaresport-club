About The Club

MORE THAN
A GAME.

UTAR Esport Club brings students together through competitive, casual and creative gaming — building teams, developing skills and creating a stronger gaming community.

Who We Are

ONE CLUB. EVERY PLAYER.

UTAR Esport Club is a student-focused gaming community where players can compete, practise, socialise and grow together. The club supports both competitive rosters and members who simply enjoy gaming with friends.

Our website brings the community together in one place, allowing visitors to explore teams, discover players, follow tournaments and check upcoming events.
